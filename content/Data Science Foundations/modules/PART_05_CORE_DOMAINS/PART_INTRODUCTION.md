# Part V: Core Domains

## Introduction

Data science isn't a single skill; it's a toolbox that can be applied to many different worlds. In this part, we explore the most impactful domains where AI is changing how we live and work.

We will dive into the specific challenges and architectures of:

1. **Computer Vision**: Teaching machines to see and understand images.
2. **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**: Mastering the complexities of human speech and text.
3. **Time Series Analysis**: Predicting the future patterns of time-dependent data.
4. **Recommendation Systems**: The algorithms that power the "personalized" internet.
5. **Generative AI**: The frontier of models that create new content from scratch.

### Chapters in this Part

- **Chapter 21: Computer Vision Basics** — Preprocessing, detection, and segmentation.
- **Chapter 22: NLP Basics** — Tokenization, embeddings, and sentiment.
- **Chapter 23: Time Series Basics** — Trends, seasonality, and ARIMA.
- **Chapter 24: Recommendation Systems** — Collaborative filtering and ranking.
- **Chapter 25: Introduction to Generative AI** — GANs, Diffusion, and LLMs.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, you will:
1. Understand the specialized preprocessing required for different data types.
2. Be able to build a basic object detector or text classifier.
3. Handle the "Curse of Dimensionality" in high-dimensional text data.
4. Grasp the intuition behind the newest generative models.
