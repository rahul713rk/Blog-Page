## Introduction

In the previous parts, we built our laboratory (Part I) and sharpened our tools (Part II). Now, we begin the work of building models that can see, predict, and categorize the world.

Machine learning is broadly divided into how models are taught. In this part, we explore the algorithms that form the backbone of modern data science:

1. **Supervised Learning**: Teaching a model with labeled examples (like Regression and Classification).
2. **Unsupervised Learning**: Letting the model find patterns on its own (like Clustering and PCA).
3. **Model Evaluation**: Learning how to tell if our model is actually "smart" or just memorizing the data.

### Chapters in this Part

- **Chapter 11: Introduction to Machine Learning** — Supervised, Unsupervised, and RL.
- **Chapter 12: Regression Algorithms** — Predicting numbers.
- **Chapter 13: Classification Algorithms** — Categorizing outcomes.
- **Chapter 14: Clustering and Dimensionality Reduction** — Finding hidden groups.
- **Chapter 15: Model Evaluation** — Precision, Recall, and ROI.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, you will:
1. Know which algorithm to choose for any tabular dataset.
2. Be able to build, train, and test models using Scikit-Learn.
3. Understand the "Bias-Variance Tradeoff" and how to fight overfitting.
4. Scale your analysis to multi-class and multi-label problems.
