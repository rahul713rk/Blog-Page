## Introduction

Knowing how to build a model is only 20% of the job. The remaining 80% is the "Engineering"—moving data at scale, writing efficient SQL, deploying your model so users can actually use it, and ensuring your AI is ethical and safe.

In this final part, we move from the notebook to the "Production" environment. We will cover:

1. **Data Pipelines**: Building robust "pipes" (ETL) to move data from databases to models.
2. **SQL**: Mastering the language of business data.
3. **Deployment**: Using Docker and APIs to turn a Python script into a live service.
4. **Ethics**: Navigating the bias, privacy, and safety challenges of the AI era.
5. **Final Projects**: Applying everything you've learned to build real-world applications.

### Chapters in this Part

- **Chapter 26: Data Pipelines** — ETL, Airflow, and Data Lakes.
- **Chapter 27: SQL for Data Science** — Joins, Window Functions, and Performance.
- **Chapter 28: Deployment Basics** — Flask/FastAPI, Docker, and CI/CD.
- **Chapter 29: Ethics and Responsible AI** — Bias, Fairness, and Explainability.
- **Chapter 30: Final Projects** — Capstones in CV, NLP, and Recommendation.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, you will:
1. Be able to orchestrate a complex data workflow from end-to-end.
2. Write production-grade SQL for massive datasets.
3. Package your model into a Container that runs anywhere.
4. Lead discussions on AI ethics and bias mitigation.
5. Have a portfolio-ready project showcasing your end-to-end skills.
