## Introduction

Welcome to the era of "Deep Learning." In this part, we transition from classical algorithms to **Neural Networks**—computational systems loosely inspired by the structure of the human brain.

Deep learning is the technology responsible for recent breakthroughs in computer vision, language translation, and generative AI. We will explore:

1. **Neural Architectures**: How the Perceptron evolved into the Deep Feedforward Network.
2. **Frameworks**: Mastering PyTorch and TensorFlow for GPU-accelerated training.
3. **Specialized Layers**: Convolutions for vision and sequence models (RNN/LSTM) for time-series and text.
4. **The Transformer Revolution**: Understanding the architecture that changed the world of NLP.

### Chapters in this Part

- **Chapter 16: Neural Networks** — Perceptrons, Backpropagation, and Activations.
- **Chapter 17: Deep Learning Frameworks** — PyTorch, TensorFlow, and GPU training.
- **Chapter 18: CNN Fundamentals** — Convolutions, Pooling, and Image Classification.
- **Chapter 19: Sequence Models** — RNNs, LSTMs, and GRUs.
- **Chapter 20: Transformers Introduction** — Attention, Mutli-Head Attention, and GPT.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, you will:
1. Be able to build and train a multi-layer neural network from scratch.
2. Understand the mathematical "black box" of backpropagation.
3. Use pre-trained models for Transfer Learning in Computer Vision.
4. Grasp the "Attention" mechanism that powers modern LLMs.
