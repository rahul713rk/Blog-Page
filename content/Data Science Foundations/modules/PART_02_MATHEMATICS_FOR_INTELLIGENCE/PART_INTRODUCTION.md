## Introduction

Mathematics is the invisible engine of Artificial Intelligence. While high-level libraries allow you to train models with a single line of code, understanding the "why" requires a journey into the world of numbers, distributions, and transformations.

In this part, we explore the four pillars of machine learning math:

1. **Statistics**: Quantifying uncertainty and finding patterns.
2. **Linear Algebra**: The language of multi-dimensional data.
3. **Calculus**: The logic of optimization and learning.
4. **Information Theory**: Measuring how much "knowledge" is in our data.

### Chapters in this Part

- **Chapter 7: Statistics Essentials** — From mean/median to the Central Limit Theorem.
- **Chapter 8: Linear Algebra for ML** — Vectors, Matrices, and SVD.
- **Chapter 9: Calculus for ML** — Gradients, Chain Rule, and Optimization.
- **Chapter 10: Information Theory Basics** — Entropy, Cross-Entropy, and Divergence.

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this part, you will:
1. Speak the mathematical language used in research papers.
2. Understand exactly how models "learn" by following gradients.
3. Be able to interpret the output of statistical tests.
4. Grasp the geometric interpretation of high-dimensional data.
